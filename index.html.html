Why Aldeara exists

The big platforms charge caregivers to be seen and match on almost nothing. Aldeara does the opposite. Caregivers are never charged. Families and caregivers match on values, care philosophy, and how a home actually feels — and families only pay once they've found someone worth talking to.

How it works

Matched on what actually matters.

1

Build your profile

Answer 30+ questions across logistics, values, care philosophy, and personality. Screen time rules, discipline approach, independence, faith, communication style — all of it. It takes about 10 minutes, and every question shapes who you see.

2

Match on the non-negotiables

Hard filters handle the dealbreakers silently — faith expectations, LGBTQ+ affirmation, comfort with firearms or work-from-home parents — so a mismatch never shows up in your queue. You see your strongest matches first.

3

Connect directly

Move through your matches, read their prompts and references, and start a conversation with the ones who fit. No algorithm hand-picks for you — a nanny's judgment is built into what the platform decides matters.
