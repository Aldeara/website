Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026  ·  Aldeara LLC, New Hampshire

Our promise, in plain language: We will never sell your data. We only share your information within the account ecosystem you have explicitly set up. We don't use your data for advertising. We don't share it with data brokers. We collect only what we need to make the product work for you.

1. Who This Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to all products and services operated by Aldeara LLC, including Daily, Portfolio, and Match, as well as our websites. When we say "Aldeara," "we," "us," or "our," we mean Aldeara LLC, a New Hampshire limited liability company.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly, information generated by your use of our products, and technical information from your device.

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password
  • Profile information: photos, biographical information, professional credentials
  • Content you log: entries, notes, photos, and other records you create within the product
  • Payment information: billing details collected and processed by Stripe (we do not store your full card number)
  • Communications: messages you send through our apps or to us directly

Information generated by your use of the product

  • Activity logs: records of actions taken within the app (entries created, features used)
  • Device information: device type, operating system, browser type
  • Usage patterns: how you interact with the product, to help us improve it

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise location data
  • We do not track you across other websites or apps
  • We do not build advertising profiles

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Provide and operate the services you've signed up for
  • Sync your data across devices and with connected account members (caregiver, second parent, etc.)
  • Process payments through Stripe
  • Send you transactional emails: account confirmation, password reset, subscription receipts
  • Provide customer support when you contact us
  • Improve our products — we look at aggregate, anonymized usage patterns, not individual user data
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data to train AI or machine learning models. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your data. Ever. To anyone. For any reason.

Your information is shared only in these limited circumstances:

Within your connected account ecosystem

The fundamental purpose of Aldeara products is connection between trusted people — a family and their caregiver, a caregiver and their clients. Information you enter is shared within the ecosystem you set up: the people you have explicitly invited and connected to your account. No one outside that connection can access your data.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate our products:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Your data is stored on their infrastructure.
  • Stripe — payment processing. We share only what's necessary to process your subscription.
  • Email providers — to send transactional emails (verification, receipts, invites).

These providers are contractually prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than providing services to us.

Legal requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public. We will notify you if we receive such a request, unless we are legally prohibited from doing so.

Business transfer

If Aldeara LLC is acquired, merges, or transfers its assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before that happens and your data will remain subject to the protections in this policy.

5. Data Specific to Each Product

Daily — Childcare Tracker

Daily is designed for families and their caregivers. It collects and stores sensitive information about children including names, dates of birth, health information (allergies, medications, feeding, sleep, and development records), and photos.

This information is only accessible to the primary family account and the connected caregiver and/or second parent accounts that the family has explicitly invited. We treat children's health data with the highest level of care and do not use it for any purpose other than providing the service to you.

Daily is not directed at children. If you believe a child has created an account without parental consent, please contact us at aldeara@proton.me immediately.

Portfolio — Caregiver Professional Tools

Portfolio collects professional information including your biography, certifications, work history, references, client information, hours worked, mileage logs, and financial records (for invoicing purposes).

Your public portfolio page (if you create one) is intentionally visible to anyone with the link — that is its purpose. All other information (client records, hours, finances, documents) is private and only visible to you.

Match — Caregiver Marketplace

Match facilitates connections between families and caregivers. Profile information you make public in Match is visible to other Match users. Private messages between users are not reviewed by Aldeara except where required for safety or legal compliance.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law (for example, financial records related to payment transactions).

Backups may take up to 90 days to fully purge after account deletion.

7. Security

We take security seriously. Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We use Supabase's row-level security to ensure that each user can only access data they are authorized to see. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

No system is perfectly secure. We will notify you promptly if we discover a breach that affects your data.

8. Children's Privacy

Our account-creation services are intended for adults (18 and older). Daily is designed to help adults care for and track information about children in their care — it is not intended to be used by children themselves.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 as account holders. If we learn we have done so, we will delete that information promptly.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request that we delete your account and personal data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to processing of your data in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, email us at aldeara@proton.me. We will respond within 30 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

If you are a resident of California, Virginia, Colorado, or another state with specific data privacy rights, those rights apply to you in addition to the above.

10. Cookies and Tracking

Our web apps use session cookies necessary for authentication and to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. We do not use Google Analytics or similar tracking services.

11. Changes to This Policy

When we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you by email or through the app at least 14 days before the changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this document will always reflect when it was last updated.

We will always keep the previous version accessible so you can see what changed.

12. Contact

Privacy questions, data requests, or concerns — we read every email and respond promptly.
